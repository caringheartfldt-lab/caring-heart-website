ACCESSIBILITY

A welcoming website for everyone.

We want people of different abilities, ages, languages, and devices to be able to find and use information about Caring Heart Foundation.

Our commitment

Caring Heart Foundation is committed to working toward a website that is inclusive and easier to use. We aim to make our online information about programs, events, volunteering, and ways to contact the Foundation accessible to as many people as reasonably possible. Accessibility is an ongoing effort: this statement describes our goals and practices, and is not a certification that every page or feature currently meets a particular technical standard.

Accessibility standards and guidance

When reviewing and improving this Website, we use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) as a technical reference. WCAG is organized around four principles: content should be perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust. We use these principles to guide improvements, with WCAG 2.2 Level AA as a useful development target where appropriate. This statement does not represent a completed audit or guarantee of conformance. Applicable legal requirements depend on the organization and its activities.

Perceivable

Provide readable text, meaningful image descriptions, adequate contrast, and alternatives for important audio or video information where possible.

Operable

Work toward navigation and controls that can be used with a keyboard and other assistive technologies, with clear focus indicators and enough time to read content.

Understandable

Use clear headings, plain language where practical, predictable navigation, descriptive links, and helpful form labels and instructions.

Robust

Use semantic HTML and common web practices so pages can work with a range of current browsers, devices, and assistive technologies.

Features and practices we aim to support

  • Keyboard access: important navigation links and controls should be reachable and usable without a mouse, where the feature supports it.
  • Screen readers: page structure, headings, links, buttons, and images should have meaningful names or descriptions where needed.
  • Text size and zoom: content should remain readable and usable when visitors enlarge text or zoom in, to the extent supported by the page and browser.
  • Color and contrast: text and important controls should be distinguishable from their backgrounds; color alone should not be the only way to convey important information.
  • Images and graphics: informative images should have useful alternative text; decorative images should not create unnecessary screen-reader clutter.
  • Video and audio: we aim to provide captions, transcripts, or other alternatives for relevant media where feasible. Some third-party media may have accessibility features controlled by the provider.
  • Mobile and responsive layouts: pages should adapt to different screen sizes without requiring unnecessary horizontal scrolling for ordinary content.
  • Language and navigation: the Website offers English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese options. We aim to make language controls and navigation labels clear and consistent.
  • Forms and contact methods: where forms are provided, labels and instructions should be clear, and errors should be explained in a helpful way where possible.

Using your device's accessibility features

You may be able to improve your experience using features already available on your device or browser, such as screen readers, magnification, browser zoom, increased text size, captions, voice control, or high-contrast settings. The names and availability of these features vary by device, operating system, and browser.

Ongoing review and known limitations

We intend to review accessibility as pages and features are created or updated. Some content may be provided by third parties, such as embedded videos, maps, documents, or external websites, and may not be fully controlled by the Foundation. Older documents, photographs containing text, or media without captions may also present barriers. If you encounter a problem, please let us know so we can assess it and consider an appropriate alternative or correction.

Report an accessibility issue or request assistance

If you have difficulty accessing any page, reading content, using a control, completing a form, or accessing a document or video, please contact us through the Contact page. If possible, tell us the page address, what you were trying to do, what happened, and which device or browser you were using. Please do not include passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive personal information in your report.

Go to our Contact page

We will review accessibility feedback and work to respond or provide an alternative way to obtain information where reasonably possible. We do not publish a fixed response time in this statement; please contact us if your request is time-sensitive.
