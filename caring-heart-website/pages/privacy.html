PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy matters to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how Caring Heart Foundation handles information collected through this website and related communications.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Who we are

Caring Heart Foundation is a community-focused nonprofit organization serving older adults and the broader community in Central Florida. Our website provides information about our programs, events, volunteer opportunities, community activities and ways to support our work.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may receive information such as:

  • Contact information, such as your name, email address, phone number or mailing address, when you voluntarily provide it.
  • Volunteer, event-registration or program-related information that you choose to submit.
  • Donation-related information needed to acknowledge or administer a contribution. Payment-card information may be collected directly by a third-party payment processor rather than by this website.
  • Information you send to us by email, telephone or other communication methods.
  • Technical information that may be provided automatically by a web host, content-delivery service or browser, such as IP address, device type, browser type, approximate location and access times.

3. How we use information

We may use information to respond to inquiries, communicate about programs and events, administer volunteer or participant requests, process or acknowledge donations, provide requested information or services, maintain and improve the website, protect the website and organization, and comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

4. How we share information

We do not sell or rent personal information for money. We may share information when reasonably necessary to operate the website and organization, including with service providers that help with website hosting, forms, communications, event registration, donation processing, information technology or similar functions. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or when you direct us to share it.

5. Donations and third-party services

If donation, payment, registration, video, social-media or other features are provided through a third-party service, that service may collect and process information under its own privacy policy and terms. Please review the applicable third party's privacy notices before submitting information. Caring Heart Foundation does not ask you to send payment-card numbers, passwords or other sensitive credentials by ordinary email.

6. Cookies and local browser storage

The current website uses browser local storage to remember your language preference and light/dark mode preference. These settings are stored in your browser and are not used by the website to identify you. The website may also receive standard technical information from its hosting or delivery infrastructure. If we later add analytics, advertising, registration, donation or other tracking technologies, this policy should be updated to describe those technologies and the choices available to visitors.

7. Information about health and care services

Some Caring Heart Foundation programs may provide general education, community resources or navigation related to health, insurance or care. Unless a specific service is separately identified and governed by an applicable privacy notice, please do not submit medical records, diagnoses, insurance identification numbers or other highly sensitive health information through a general website form or ordinary email. When a third-party provider handles a service, its privacy practices may also apply.

8. Data retention and security

We seek to keep personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, legitimate organizational needs, or legal and recordkeeping requirements. We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. No method of transmitting or storing information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your choices and privacy requests

You may contact us to ask what personal information we maintain about you, request correction of inaccurate information, ask us to delete information where appropriate, or ask questions about how information is used. Some information may need to be retained because of legal, accounting, security or other legitimate requirements. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

10. Children and families

Our website is intended to provide information about community programs and services and is not directed primarily to children. Parents or guardians should supervise minors' use of the website. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us in a way that should not have occurred, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, technology or legal obligations change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.

12. Contact us about privacy

Privacy questions or requests may be sent to Caring Heart Foundation at 636 Wilmer Ave., Orlando, FL 32808, by telephone at 866-282-6888, or by email at CaringHeartFL@gmail.com.

Important launch note

This policy is a website draft based on the current Caring Heart Foundation site structure and stated activities. Before public launch, the Foundation should review it with qualified legal/privacy counsel and update it to match the actual hosting provider, donation processor, forms, email service, analytics, cookies, databases and any other third-party services actually used.
