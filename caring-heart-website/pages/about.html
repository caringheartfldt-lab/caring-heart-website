ABOUT US

A caring community where every season of life matters.

Caring Heart Foundation is a newly established nonprofit focused on improving quality of life for aging adults in Central Florida through connection, learning and practical support.

OUR MISSION

Dignity. Purpose. Joy.

We envision a sanctuary where seniors can live with dignity, purpose and joy through a comprehensive support system.

Our work is rooted in the belief that aging is not a loss of possibility. It can be a season of learning, friendship, contribution and renewed purpose.

WHAT GUIDES US

A simple idea: care should feel human.

💜

Our Vision

A Central Florida community where older adults are connected, respected and able to continue growing.

💚

Our Values

Compassion, dignity, inclusion, lifelong learning, practical support and community partnership.

🌱

Our Approach

Listen first, connect people with appropriate resources, create welcoming programs and grow responsibly with the community.
