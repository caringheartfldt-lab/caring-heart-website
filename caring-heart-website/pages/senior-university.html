CARING HEART PROGRAM

🎓 Senior University

Lifelong learning and social engagement for older adults, beginning with Phase I in 2026.

Learn something new, together

The first phase is designed as a welcoming learning community rather than a traditional classroom.

  • Calligraphy & painting
  • Smartphone applications
  • Health & wellness
  • English conversation
  • Interest groups

Why it matters

Learning, creativity and friendship can help older adults stay connected, confident and engaged.

Future opportunities

The program can expand into additional classes, guest speakers, field trips, technology help and intergenerational activities.
